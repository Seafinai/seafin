# Seafin homepage artwork: prompts

Style chosen 2026-10-05: editorial still-life photographs of real small-business work.
Generate in ChatGPT. Paste the shared style line after every prompt so the set matches.

**Shared style line (append to every prompt):**

> Editorial still-life photograph. Soft natural daylight from the left, gentle shadows. Warm-neutral white and light grey surfaces. Muted colours, with deep indigo (#1A227E) and teal (#0076A3) only in one or two props. Medium-format look, 50mm, shallow depth of field. Calm, uncluttered, generous empty space. No people, no hands, no logos, no readable text, no interface on any screen, no robots, no glowing or sci-fi effects.
