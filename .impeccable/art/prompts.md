# Seafin homepage artwork: prompts

Style chosen 2026-10-05: editorial still-life photographs of real small-business work.
Generated in ChatGPT. The opening photo (`hero.webp`) is from the first set; the eight
"What we build" pictures are the second set (2026-10-05), one real setting per build so
they don't read as the same desk eight times.

**Shared style line (append to every prompt):**

> Editorial still-life photograph for a professional consulting website. Soft natural daylight, gentle shadows, muted warm-neutral tones. One or two props in deep charcoal grey (#292D32) or cobalt blue (#3563E9), nothing else brightly coloured. 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, calm and uncluttered with generous empty space. 4:3 landscape. No people, no hands, no logos, no readable text, letters or numbers anywhere (paper and screens show only soft blurred shapes), no robots, no glowing or sci-fi effects.
