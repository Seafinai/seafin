Questions

How is this different from using ChatGPT?

ChatGPT helps one person with one task at a time. We connect AI to the systems your business runs on, so the work happens inside your process, with a person approving where it matters.

Is our data used to train AI models?

Not in the setups we build. Anthropic’s commercial terms say it doesn’t train its models on business customers’ data. For data that can’t leave your building, we run open-source models on your own servers.

What does it cost to run once it’s built?

Mostly model usage, which for a small business is usually tens to a few hundred dollars a month. The audit estimates it from your own volumes before you commit.

Do we need to buy new software?

Usually not. We build on what you already use, such as QuickBooks, HubSpot, Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace.

Who owns what you build?

You do: the code, the prompts and the workflows. No lock-in.

What if the audit finds nothing worth building?

Then the report says so, and it’s yours to keep. You don’t have to buy anything else.

How long until something is working?

The audit takes a week. Most builds take 2 to 6 weeks once the scope is agreed.
