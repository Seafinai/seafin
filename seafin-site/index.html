Who is this for?

Businesses of 5 to 100 people with nobody whose job is AI, where hours every week go to typing, copying, chasing or sorting. Not a fit if you want AI deciding with no person checking.

How is this different from using ChatGPT?

ChatGPT helps one person with one task at a time. We connect AI to the systems your business runs on, so it works in your inbox, accounting and scheduling software, and a person approves before anything is sent.

Is our data used to train AI models?

Not by default. Anthropic and OpenAI don’t train on business accounts’ data unless you opt in. For sensitive work, the AI can run inside your own Amazon, Microsoft or Google cloud account instead.

What does it cost to run once it’s built?

Mostly what the AI companies charge for use, which for a small business is usually tens to a few hundred dollars a month. The audit estimates it from your own volumes before you commit.

Who owns what you build?

You do: the code, prompts and workflows we build for you. If any of our own reusable tools are inside, you get a permanent licence to use and change them, so you can keep running it, or hand it to someone else, without us.

What if the audit finds nothing worth building?

Then the report says so. If it finds fewer than 3 opportunities, each worth more than the audit fee a year, we refund the fee in full. If it finds 3 or more and you go ahead within 90 days, the fee comes off the build price.
