FAQ

Common questions.

01 Who is Seafin built for? +
Businesses without dedicated AI staff — solo operators through ~500-person teams. If nobody in-house owns AI, you've heard about Claude but aren't sure where to start, or you've signed up and gotten stuck, we're the team that makes it actually work. Any industry; we don't require deep vertical expertise from you.
02 How long does a project take? +
Audit: 1 week from kickoff to written report. Builds: 2–6 weeks from contract to deployed, depending on scope. Most workflows ship in 2–3 weeks; multi-system integrations run 4–6. Concierge: live within 1 week of signing. You see weekly demos during every build — no surprises at the end.
03 Is my data secure? +
Yes. Enterprise-grade encryption at rest and in transit, role-based access, audit logging. Cloud APIs are governed by provider security terms (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google). For complete sovereignty or air-gapped environments, we deploy local models on your infrastructure.
04 Will it work with our existing tools? +
Almost certainly. We integrate Claude with QuickBooks, HubSpot, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Slack, Salesforce, Shopify, and many more — via Claude for Small Business connectors or custom MCP servers. During discovery we map your full stack.
05 Why Claude specifically? +
Anthropic shipped the strongest packaged SMB workflow product on the market with Claude for Small Business, and their partner economics are accessible to small consultancies. We deploy Claude as the anchor and reach for ChatGPT, Gemini, or Codex when the job calls for it. We sell results, not platform loyalty.
06 What if we can't use cloud AI? (Sovereign / air-gapped) +
We build local LLM workflows for customers with data sovereignty, compliance, or air-gap requirements. Models: Llama 3.3, Qwen 2.5, Mistral, DeepSeek. Inference via vLLM, Ollama, or llama.cpp with quantization. Runs on your on-prem GPU server, private cloud, or hybrid. Open-weight models are Haiku-class today — we'll tell you when local works and when cloud is the right call. Custom-scoped, typically $25K–$150K + GPU infra at cost.
