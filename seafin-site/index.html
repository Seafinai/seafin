The audit

One week. About 2½ hours of yours.

Most of the work is ours. The working session with the person who does the job is where the steps nobody wrote down come out.

Day 1 · 20 min

A short form

Ten questions for you, three for two or three of your staff. We learn your goals, your tools and the jobs that repeat.

Day 3 · 90 min

Working session

The person who does the job shows us the last time they did it. We get the real steps, timed and scored.

Day 5 · 30 min

Your plan

We walk you through it and answer questions. You get the top three changes, a 90-day plan and a fixed price for the first one.

FAQ

Common questions.

01 Who is Seafin built for? +
Teams of 10 to 200 where the same admin jobs repeat every week and nobody on staff has time to work out AI. Most run on Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, QuickBooks or HubSpot. With fewer than 5 people, Seafin Personal is a better fit. If you already have an in-house AI team, you probably don't need us, and we'll say so on the first call.
02 What if you can't find 5 hours a week? +
You get your $499 back. The guarantee is tied to what the audit finds: if we can't show you at least five hours a week your team can get back, you don't pay.
03 How long does a project take? +
Audit: 1 week from kickoff to written report. Builds: 2–6 weeks from contract to deployed, depending on scope. Most workflows ship in 2–3 weeks; multi-system integrations run 4–6. Concierge: live within 1 week of signing. You see a demo every week of a build, so nothing is a surprise at the end.
04 Where does our data go? +
Every change in your plan says where your data goes and whose terms cover it. We use business accounts in your name, so you hold the keys and see the bills, and access stays with the people who need it. If your data can't leave the building, we can run models on your own hardware.
05 Will it work with our existing tools? +
Almost certainly. We connect AI to QuickBooks, HubSpot, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Slack, Salesforce, Shopify and more, using built-in connectors where they exist and custom ones where they don't. The audit maps every tool the job touches.
06 Which AI do you use? +
Whatever fits the job and your budget. We're not tied to any AI company. Claude is what we use most, because it's strong at the writing and document work that admin is made of. When ChatGPT, Gemini or a tool you already pay for fits better, that's what we recommend.
07 What if we can't use cloud AI? (Sovereign / air-gapped) +
We build local LLM workflows for customers with data sovereignty, compliance, or air-gap requirements. Models: Llama 3.3, Qwen 2.5, Mistral, DeepSeek. Inference via vLLM, Ollama, or llama.cpp with quantization. Runs on your on-prem GPU server, private cloud, or hybrid. Open models still trail the best cloud models, so we'll tell you honestly when local works and when cloud is the right call. Custom-scoped, typically $25K–$150K + GPU infra at cost.
